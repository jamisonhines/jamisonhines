# Hey, I'm Jaymo

I build real systems with AI, and I love teaching other people to do the same.

I'm not a traditional engineer and I don't pretend to be. I'm an AI-native builder. I work with AI to design, build and ship software, and what I love most is taking a process that lives in spreadsheets, notes and people's heads and turning it into something that just handles itself.

Before any of this I taught for seven years. Close to 4,000 paid one to one English lessons, mostly with Russian-speaking software engineers, plus a decade of teaching snowboarding on a mountain. Explaining a hard thing plainly to someone who did not ask for a lecture is the skill I have practiced most, and it turns out to be most of the job.

These days I run a tour company and a ski school in Georgia (the country) on software I built, and I handle around 60 client emails a week in season through a system that drafts most of them for me.

Most of my code is private because it runs live businesses, so the pinned repos are build logs rather than source. What each system does, how it's put together, where it's headed. I update them as things move.

## What I'm building

- **[Vagabond OPS](https://github.com/jamisonhines/vagabond-ops-case-study)**, operations software for my adventure tour company. CRM, pricing engine and quote builder, vendor management, expense tracking, participant waivers. Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, row-level security on all 66 tables, 4,203 automated tests.
- **[AIOS](https://github.com/jamisonhines/aios-case-study)**, nine specialist AI agents wrapped around a personal knowledge base. Triages my business inbox, writes my morning brief, does the monthly bookkeeping, and files what it learns back into plain markdown files. Over 450 recorded failure lessons, each one routed somewhere that stops it repeating.
- **[AI Email Workflow](https://github.com/jamisonhines/email-workflow-case-study)**, triages my business inbox three times a day into 24 categories, drafts on-brand replies and quotes from the company wiki for a human to approve, and improves itself nightly by comparing its drafts against what actually got sent. It cannot send on its own, and it re-proves that before every run.
- **[Changa La Gungei](https://github.com/jamisonhines/changa-la-gungei-case-study)**, a language learning game built on full immersion and spaced retrieval. I'm learning Georgian myself, so I'm building the tool I need.
- **[aios-dashboard](https://github.com/jamisonhines/aios-dashboard)**, an Obsidian plugin, public code, that turns my project files into a live task dashboard.

## How I work

Day to day I live in Claude Code and AI tooling, including local models for the bulk work that doesn't need a frontier model. The stack underneath is Next.js, React, TypeScript and Supabase. I pick up new tools and domains fast, and I'm honest about what I am. I direct AI to write the code, and I own the design, the data, and whether the thing actually works for the people using it.

The habit I'd point at is that I measure my own claims. Every number on these pages gets re-counted from the system before it goes up, and a few of them have moved against me when I did that.
